Respondent consent

The DPDP notice read to the respondent (the informant) before their details are saved. When required, the web shows it at death registration, before the informant's details, and before an interview whose case has no consent decision yet. Editing a notice saves a new version; consent records keep the version they were shown. A version is pending legal review until an admin approves it; it can be used either way.
