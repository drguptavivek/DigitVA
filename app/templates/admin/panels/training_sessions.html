Training sessions

Each session is one training: each coder slot holds 10 pseudonymised cases, and you choose the number of slots per session (one per coder). The source has about 200 cases, so beyond 20 slots the same case is copied into more than one slot. Trainees sign in with their own accounts; add existing users below. Closing a session deactivates its participants' training access and deletes nothing.
