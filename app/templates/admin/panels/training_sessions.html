Training sessions

Each session is one training of one type. An interviewer session has no coder slots and no cases. In a coder or self-coding session each coder takes a slot and gets their own random 10 pseudonymised cases of the English training set when added, so different coders' sets may overlap. Self-coding coders also interview and code their own interviews. Older sessions without a type keep their mixed roles and the cases copied when they were created. Trainees sign in with their own accounts; add existing users below, or import a file that also creates missing accounts. Closing a session deactivates its participants' training access and deletes nothing; practice interviews are kept.
