COD Bucket Reporting

The COD Bucket Reporting page at /data-management/cod-buckets provides cause-of-death aggregates by grouping individual ICD-coded submissions into broader reporting categories (buckets). This is a reporting-only layer: it does not modify the authoritative final COD stored on each submission.

Key principle: Changing a bucket mapping or reporting scheme never rewrites a coder's or reviewer's final COD record. The reporting layer is versioned separately from coding data.

What Are COD Buckets?

A COD bucket is a grouped category that maps one or more ICD-10 codes to a broader cause-of-death label for public health reporting. For example, individual ICD codes for different types of heart disease can be grouped into a single "Cardiovascular Diseases" bucket. Each mapping connects an ICD code (and optionally an age scope) to a leaf-level bucket node in a scheme hierarchy.

Available Schemes

Scheme Age Scope Hierarchy Depth
SRS India Age-scoped: adult_over5y, child_1_59m, neonate Category → Subcategory → Disease
CMEA10 No age scope (flat) Disease only
WHO 2022 VA Flat all-ages scope WHO VA Section → WHO VA Cause Bucket

The page defaults to the WHO 2022 VA scheme when it is available.

Filters

The reporting page supports the following filters:

  • Scheme — choose which COD bucket scheme to display
  • Project — filter to a specific project within your scope
  • Site — filter to a specific site within your scope
  • Form — filter to a specific ODK form
  • Submission date range — narrow results to a date window
  • Gender — filter by All, Male, Female, or Unknown
Scoping: All data is scoped to your data-manager grants. You only see projects and sites you have been explicitly granted access to. Form filters are a convenience within your allowed scope and cannot expand visibility beyond it.

Dashboard Sections

Key Indicators

At the top, the page shows summary indicators: total coded submissions in scope, the active reporting scheme, and the current filter context.

Top-10 Causes

A ranked table and horizontal bar chart showing the ten most frequent causes of death for the filtered population. This section uses the matched, in-scope, authoritative final-ICD reporting population.

Cause-Group Counts

First-level cause-group counts with percentages, giving an overview of how deaths distribute across major categories in the selected scheme.

Demographic Summaries

Three demographic visualizations for the filtered population:

  • Age-sex table — cross-tabulation of age bands against sex
  • Age pyramid — population pyramid showing age distribution by sex
  • Sex pie chart — proportional breakdown by sex

Age bands for demographic summaries depend on the selected scheme:

  • SRS India uses the scheme's own defined age bands
  • WHO 2022 VA and CMEA10 use fixed bands: 0-<28 days, 28 days-<365 days, 365 days-<12 years, 12 years-<50 years, >=50 years

Heatmap

A top-10 cause heatmap that pivots across country, year, sex, and age dimensions. Country is derived from the form's SmartVA country field. Year uses the submission date year. Sex uses normalized deceased gender. Age uses the demographic age-band policy.

Treemap

An all-causes treemap visualization for the same filtered matched population, showing proportional distribution of all bucketed causes.

Detailed COD Hierarchy Tables

The bottom section shows the full scheme hierarchy with the following columns for each row: Male n (%), Female n (%), Unknown n (%), and Total n (%).

  • The "All Ages" table appears first
  • For WHO 2022 VA and CMEA10, additional detailed tables follow for each reporting age band
  • Tables follow the scheme-defined sort order, not label-based sorting

If coded submissions have a final ICD that does not map to any active node in the selected scheme for a given age group, a note appears beneath that table showing how many CODs were dropped. The note includes a link to a modal with two sections:

  • ICD codes not included in the selected scheme's categories for that age group
  • ICD codes not eligible for coding

CSV Export

The page supports CSV export of the current scoped data. The export includes SID, age, age group, sex, final authoritative COD, scheme hierarchy levels, project, site, form code, and year. Exports default to aggregate outputs, not row-level submission dumps.

PII protection: CSV exports are designed to avoid exposing personally identifiable information. Exports contain coded cause-of-death data and demographic aggregates, not raw submission payloads or narrative text.

Printing

The reporting page is styled for printing and does not rely on internal card or table scrolling to expose data. All summary and detailed sections render fully when printed.

Admin COD Bucket Editor

Administrators can manage reporting schemes at /admin/panels/cod-buckets. This panel supports:

  • Selecting a scheme and age scope from top-level scheme cards
  • Exporting any scheme as JSON or XLSX
  • Importing an exported JSON snapshot back into a scheme (fully replaces age bands, nodes, and mappings)
  • Creating new schemes with age bands, bounds, units, and level count
  • Editing scheme names, age-band metadata, and display sort order
  • Resetting built-in schemes (SRS India, CMEA10, WHO 2022 VA) to their migration-artifact defaults
  • Adding or removing ICD mappings at the disease-leaf level via an ICD search modal
  • Bulk-allocating unmapped ICD codes to a target age band and disease leaf
  • Viewing a scheme-wide unmapped ICD grid with finalized COD and SmartVA usage counts
  • Deleting bucket nodes with a choice to unmap or move affected ICD codes to an "Unmapped" replacement
Mapping uniqueness: Within a scheme, each ICD code maps to at most one active target leaf for a given age scope. The ICD picker in the admin editor searches the ICD-10 2019 master table and shows codes regardless of coding allowability, with a "Currently not assignable in coding" marker for restricted codes.

COD Snapshot Export

The COD Snapshot Export captures a point-in-time snapshot of final COD authority for analysis. It is a submission-granular export (one row per va_sid) that reports only active/current COD-facing records.

The snapshot preserves three distinct human-COD views when available:

  • Latest active coder data
  • Latest active reviewer data
  • Authoritative final COD outcome

The export also includes SmartVA results, Narrative Quality Assessment scores, Social Autopsy fields, and WHO 2022 VA bucket mappings derived independently for each ICD source (coder, reviewer, authoritative, and SmartVA causes 1-3).

Scope: The COD snapshot export is scoped to your data-manager project/site grants, the same as other data-management reporting features.
