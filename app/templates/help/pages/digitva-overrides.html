WHO's releases are the baseline. DigitVA's own decisions are layered on top of them and kept separate, so you can see exactly what DigitVA decided and where it differs from WHO. An administrator applies the layers with "Apply DigitVA overrides" in the admin ICD-10 browser or the COD bucket editor. "Applied on this server" says whether this server currently matches the decision, looking only at active codes and mappings (the ones coders see). Background: the policies "WHO 2022 ICD-10 Coding Allowability Policy" and "ICD-11 COD Bucket Schemes" in the project documentation. This page is read-only.
