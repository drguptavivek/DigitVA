ICD Codes, VA Causes and the Coding Search

Try the coding search — run the real search below without opening a death.

In Step 2 of the coding workflow a coder records the cause of death as an ICD code. Every coding path ends in an ICD code; the WHO verbal autopsy causes (VAs-01.01 … VAs-99) are how those codes are grouped for reporting, not something the coder picks instead.

ICD-10 and ICD-11

DigitVA carries both classifications as local reference catalogues:

  • ICD-10 — WHO ICD-10 2019 (mas_icd10_2019_2), loaded from WHO's ClaML release: chapters, blocks, three-character categories and detailed dotted codes.
  • ICD-11 — the ICD-11 MMS 2026-01 release (mas_icd11_mms).

Each project chooses its classification: ICD-10, ICD-11, or selectable. In a selectable project the coding screen shows a Code this death in ICD-10 / ICD-11 switch; switching clears the COD fields, because a code from the other catalogue would be rejected on save. The ICD browser link on the coding screen follows the switch.

The ICD-11 selectability policy is a draft awaiting expert review. Until it is approved, treat ICD-11 coding as provisional.

VA Cause Codes

Every ICD code maps to one WHO 2022 VA cause (reporting scheme WHO_2022_VA_2026, which covers both ICD-10 and ICD-11). After you select a code, the floating VA definition panel shows the WHO definition of the cause it belongs to, and the VA Definitions button lists all causes.

  • VA Cause Definitions
  • ICD to VA Cause Mappings — every code with its VA cause, and an ICD-10 / ICD-11 side-by-side view per cause

Because reporting works on VA causes, codes that differ only in a detail a verbal autopsy cannot establish are equivalent for the report. Tuberculosis is the clearest case: bacteriological confirmation is almost never known, and A15, A16 and 1B10.x all report as Pulmonary tuberculosis. The search therefore suggests the unspecified code (A16 / 1B10.Z).

How the Coding Search Works

Type at least two characters in a COD box. Results are always limited to the codes this death may receive (see Which codes are selectable below), and appear in two columns. Try it live with an age group and sex of your choosing.

  • Focused matches (left) — the strongest candidates:
    • a clinical term you typed that DigitVA knows (shown as term — code title, e.g. MI — I21 Acute myocardial infarction);
    • an exact code (A09);
    • a title where your text begins a word (gastroen → Other gastroenteritis…).
  • Term matches (right) — every other title that contains your text. Nothing is hidden; pick from either column.

Clinical terms

ICD titles often do not use the words clinicians use: diarrhoea appears in no selectable ICD-10 title, and nobody types Respiratory tuberculosis, not confirmed bacteriologically or histologically. DigitVA keeps a reviewed list of clinical terms, shorthand and local names that point to the right code:

  • shorthand — MI, CVA, CCF, CKD, RTA, TB, PPH;
  • common names — heart attack, loose motions, enteric fever, snake bite;
  • local terms — Kochs, madhumeh, mirgi.

Terms match while you are still typing (from three characters: dysen finds dysentery); two-letter shorthand such as MI must be typed in full. When a term has several possible codes they are listed in a fixed order, most common first.

Spelling, wording and typos

  • UK and US spellings match each other (anaemia / anemia, diarrhoea / diarrhea), and hyphens and spaces are interchangeable (cat scratch / Cat-scratch).
  • Classification boilerplate is ignored when matching and ranking: Other, unspecified, NOS, not elsewhere classified, not confirmed, of unspecified origin, classified elsewhere. Titles are still shown in full.
  • If a search finds nothing at all, DigitVA tries close spellings (dysentry → dysentery). These suggestions only appear when the exact search is empty.

Injuries and external causes

WHO codes an injury death by its external cause, not by the injury. ICD-10 S00-T99 are never selectable, so a term such as head injury offers the likely external causes instead: road traffic (V89.2), fall (W19), assault (Y09), struck by a falling object (W20), or unspecified (X59). Anaphylaxis and allergic reaction likewise offer drug, vaccine, bee or wasp sting, venomous animal, or unspecified.

Transport accidents must be coded to the level that says whether the death was a road traffic accident: the three-character ICD-10 codes V01-V89 and Y85 are not selectable, because WHO separates road traffic from other transport only at the fourth character (for example V89.2, unspecified motor-vehicle accident, traffic).

Which Codes Are Selectable

Not every ICD code can be a cause of death in DigitVA. Selectable codes follow the WHO VA 2022 coding allowability rules, plus local curation:

  • Age group of the death: neonate (0 to under 28 days), infant (28 days to under 1 year), child (1 to under 12 years), adult (12 years and older). Perinatal P codes are neonate-only; maternal O codes are adult women only.
  • Sex of the deceased: C51-C58 and D25-D28 female only; C60-C63 and D29 male only; C50 both.
  • Never selectable: ICD-10 S00-T99 (injuries; code the external cause) and the ambiguous three-character transport codes above.
  • Stillbirths: in ICD-11, choose KD3B.1 (intrapartum, fresh) or KD3B.0 (antepartum, macerated); the unspecified codes are not selectable. ICD-10 has a single code, P95, for both.
A code that does not appear is not selectable for this death — wrong age group or sex, an injury code, or a code clinical review has disabled. The server re-checks the code, age group and sex when you save.

WHO ICD Browsers (Admin)

Administrators curate selectability in the ICD-10 and ICD-11 browser panels (/admin/panels/icd10-browser, /admin/panels/icd11-browser): each three-character or detailed row carries is_coding_selectable, sex_selectable, age_group_selectable and a restriction note. Colour indicators: green, the row or all its children are selectable; yellow, the row is not but at least one child is; red, nothing below is. Policies export and import as JSON or XLSX; an import overwrites — rows not in the file are reset to not selectable.

The clinical-term list is maintained in the COD Search Vocabulary admin panel: add, edit, reorder, deactivate (never delete) and export. Coders' searches are recorded anonymously so missing terms and searches that found nothing can be reviewed and added.
