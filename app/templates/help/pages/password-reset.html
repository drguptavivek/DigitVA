How to Reset Your Password

If you have forgotten your password or need to set one for the first time, follow these steps:

  1. Go to the login page.
  2. Click the "Forgot Password" link below the login form.
  3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
  4. Click "Send Reset Link".
  5. Check your inbox (and spam folder) for an email from DigitVA containing a password reset link.
  6. Click the link in the email. You will be taken to the password reset page.
  7. Enter and confirm your new password.
  8. You will be redirected to the login page to sign in with your new password.
Tip: The confirmation message always reads "If that email address is registered, we've sent a password reset link." This message appears whether or not the email exists in the system, to prevent email-address enumeration.

Reset Link Expiry

Password reset links expire after 1 hour. If your link has expired, return to the Forgot Password page and request a new one.

Warning: Reset links use stateless tokens that remain valid until they expire (1 hour). Requesting a new link does not invalidate earlier links. If you suspect a link has been compromised, change your password immediately after using a fresh link.

Rate Limiting

Password reset requests are rate-limited to prevent abuse:

Limit Scope Applies To
3 requests per hour Per IP address POST to the forgot-password endpoint
5 requests per minute Per IP address POST to the reset-password endpoint

If you exceed the limit, wait for the time window to pass and try again.

Password Strength Requirements

Your new password must meet all of the following criteria:

  • At least 12 characters long
  • At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
  • At least one digit (0–9)
  • At least one special character (such as !@#$%^&*()_+-=)
  • Both password fields must match exactly

If any rule is not met, you will see a message listing all unmet requirements.

Breach Password Check (HIBP)

DigitVA checks your chosen password against the Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) breach database using a k-anonymity lookup:

  • Only the first 5 characters of your password's SHA-1 hash are sent to the HIBP service. Your full password is never transmitted externally.
  • If your password appears in known breach data, it will be rejected with the message: "Password has been found in known breach data. Choose a different password."
  • If the HIBP service is temporarily unreachable, the password change will fail with a retryable error: "Password breach check is temporarily unavailable. Please try again."
Note: The breach check is a defense-in-depth layer. Even if your password passes the breach check, it must still meet all strength requirements listed above. The breach check may be disabled in non-production test environments only.

First-Time Users

When an administrator creates your account, you do not receive a password. Instead:

  1. You receive a verification email with a link that expires in 24 hours.
  2. After clicking the verification link, you are automatically redirected to the password reset page to set your initial password.
  3. The password you set must meet all strength and breach-check requirements described above.

See Email Verification for more details on the verification step.

Forced Password Change on First Login

After setting your password for the first time (via the email verification flow or after an admin reset), you may be required to accept terms and conditions before you can access your dashboard. Until you complete this step, you will be redirected to the password change page on every request.
