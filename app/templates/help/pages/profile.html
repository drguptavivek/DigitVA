My Profile

The My Profile page at /profile lets you view your account information, change your password, and update your display timezone. Access this page from the user menu in the navigation bar after logging in.

Viewing Your Information

The profile page loads your personal details via the API at GET /api/v1/profile/. This returns your name, email, assigned coding languages, and current timezone. You can also update your coding language preferences via PATCH /api/v1/profile/language.

Changing Your Password

Use the "Change Password" card on the profile page to update your password. You must provide:

  • Current password — to verify your identity
  • New password — must meet strength requirements
  • Confirm new password — must match the new password

Password changes are submitted via PATCH /api/v1/profile/password. The endpoint is rate-limited to 5 requests per minute.

Password Strength Requirements

Your new password must satisfy all of the following rules:

  • At least 12 characters
  • At least one uppercase letter (A-Z)
  • At least one digit (0-9)
  • At least one special character
  • The new password must differ from your current password
If any rule is not met, the system tells you which requirements are missing. All rules must pass before the password is accepted.

Breach Check (Have I Been Pwned)

After your password passes the strength rules, DigitVA checks it against the Have I Been Pwned (HIBP) breach database. If your chosen password appears in known breach data, it will be rejected and you must choose a different one.

Privacy: DigitVA never transmits your full password to the breach service. Only the first 5 characters of the password's SHA-1 hash prefix are sent to the HIBP k-anonymity API. No raw password, full hash, or account identity leaves the application.
Service unavailable: If the breach check service cannot be reached, the password change will fail with a retryable error. DigitVA does not silently bypass the breach check. Try again in a few moments.

First Login: Terms and Conditions

When you log in for the first time after your password is set (for example, after account creation or a password reset by an administrator), you may be required to accept terms and conditions before accessing your dashboard.

This happens when your account has not yet accepted the terms (tracked by the pw_reset_t_and_c flag). The system redirects you to the Terms & Conditions page at /profile/force-password-change, where you must:

  1. Read the terms and conditions, which cover data confidentiality and responsible use
  2. Check the box to confirm agreement
  3. Click "Continue" to proceed to your dashboard

Once accepted, you will not be prompted again. The acceptance is recorded on your account.

Time Zone

The profile page lets you set your preferred time zone. All timestamps displayed in the system (submission dates, coding times, sync times) will be shown in your chosen time zone.

Timezone changes are submitted via PATCH /api/v1/profile/timezone. The default timezone is Asia/Kolkata. You can select from the standard IANA timezone database.

Viewing Your Assigned Roles and Scopes

Your active grants (role assignments to specific projects and sites) determine what you can see and do in DigitVA. These grants are managed by administrators or project PIs through the access grants panel.

To understand your access:

  • Your role (coder, reviewer, data manager, site PI, project PI, admin) determines which pages and actions are available to you
  • Your scope (which projects and sites) determines whose data you can access within those pages
  • If you cannot see a project or site you expect, contact your administrator to verify your grant is active and scoped correctly

For a detailed breakdown of each role and its permissions, see User Roles & Permissions.
