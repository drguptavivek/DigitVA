What Is the Recode Window?

The recode window is a 24-hour period after a coder finalizes a Cause of Death (COD) determination. During this window, the coder may reopen the case and submit a replacement COD. After 24 hours, the window closes and the coder can no longer recode the submission.

How It Works

  1. Coder submits final COD. The submission enters coder_finalized state. The 24-hour recode timer starts.
  2. During the 24-hour window, the coder can reopen the case from their coding history table and submit a new COD. The existing finalized COD remains authoritative until the replacement is successfully saved.
  3. After 24 hours, the submission automatically transitions to reviewer_eligible. The coder can no longer recode the case.
Incomplete recode work does not replace the existing COD. The original finalized COD remains authoritative until a replacement final COD is successfully saved. If the coder starts a recode but does not finish, the original COD stands.

Supporting Artifacts During Recode

Supporting artifacts behave differently during recode compared to first-pass coding:

Artifact First-Pass Timeout Recode Session
Narrative Quality Assessment (NQA) Deactivated on timeout Persists across recode attempts
Social Autopsy Analysis Deactivated on timeout Persists across recode attempts
Final COD N/A (not yet submitted) Preserved; replaced only on successful save

NQA and Social Autopsy artifacts may be updated independently during recode, even if the VA code itself is not re-saved.

Allocation Timeout During Recode

Recode sessions follow the same 1-hour stale allocation timeout as first-pass coding and reviewer sessions. If a recode allocation becomes stale:

  • The active allocation row is deactivated.
  • Unfinished initial assessment rows are deactivated.
  • The recode episode is abandoned.
  • The currently authoritative final COD is preserved.
  • Saved NQA and Social Autopsy artifacts from the recode window are preserved.
  • The submission returns to coder_finalized.
Different timeout reset behavior: An incomplete first-pass coding session resets to ready_for_coding and all supporting artifacts are deactivated. An incomplete recode session resets to coder_finalized and the existing authoritative COD is preserved.

After the Recode Window

Once the 24-hour recode window closes:

  1. The submission transitions to reviewer_eligible.
  2. The coder can no longer reopen or recode the case.
  3. The submission becomes available for optional reviewer secondary coding.
  4. The coder’s finalized COD remains authoritative unless and until a reviewer submits a reviewer final COD that supersedes it.

Time Available to Coders

The time constraints for coders are:

Constraint Duration Effect
Allocation timeout 1 hour If the coder does not complete the coding session within 1 hour, the allocation is released and the submission returns to the pool.
Recode window 24 hours After finalization, the coder has 24 hours to change the COD. After that, the case is locked for the coder.
Recode session timeout 1 hour If a recode session is not completed within 1 hour, it is abandoned. The existing COD is preserved.

Authority Chain

The authoritative COD for a submission follows this precedence:
  1. Reviewer finalized COD (if one exists and is active)
  2. Coder finalized COD (if active and not superseded)
Initial COD (Step 1) is never the authoritative final COD. It is an intermediate working assessment only.
