A permanent practice project for learning each DigitVA role on realistic, pseudonymised cases.

  • You use your own account. Your trainer adds you to a training session with a role.
  • The cases come from consented interviews. In the text answers, names, addresses and phone numbers are replaced with made-up ones; the clinical answers are kept. Photos, documents and audio are the original consented material and may show real details: do not copy, photograph or share them.
  • Coders see only their own 10 cases; the other roles see all the cases of their training session.
  • Training work stays in the training project and is separate from real cases.
