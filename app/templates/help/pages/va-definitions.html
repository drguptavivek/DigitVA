VA Cause Definitions

The WHO verbal autopsy cause codes, titles and definitions from the manual for physician reviewers. Coders and reviewers open the same list from the VA Definitions button beside the cause of death selects. Type a cause name, a code such as VAs-01.02, or a word from a definition to filter.

When you pick an ICD code in a cause of death field, the definition of the VA cause that code maps to opens in a small panel at the bottom right of the screen. It does not block the form; close it with its × button and it stays closed until you pick another code.
