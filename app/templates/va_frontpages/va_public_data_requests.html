Consent and data requests

This page applies to the DigitVA web portal and the DigitVA Collect and Collect Plus Android apps. DigitVA staff accounts are created and managed by programme administrators: deletion is requested and the programme team confirms it before acting. Public demo accounts, which people sign up for themselves with a username, can be deleted at once by their owner. Consent changes are requested by writing to us.

Withdraw or change your consent

If you gave information about a death in your family and want it withdrawn, erased or left out of reports and research, write to us{% if org.email %} (you can use the contact form){% endif %} and quote the case number you were given. Information already shared for research in de-identified form cannot be taken back because it no longer identifies you.

Delete a public demo account

Sign in to the demo, in the app or on the website, and use Delete my account (in the app: Settings; on the website: your profile). Enter your password again to confirm. The account and everything it created are deleted at once. The account's security and sign-in audit records are kept, without its name.

Request deletion of your account and data

Signed in to the app, open Settings and use Delete my account to send a deletion request; no email or password is needed, and the app shows "Request sent". You can also:

  1. {% if org.email %}Email {{ org.email }} from the address registered on your account, use the contact form, or ask your project administrator to send the request for you. {% else %}Ask your project administrator to send the request to the programme team.{% endif %}
  2. Say that you want your account and associated data deleted, and give your name and the email address or mobile number you sign in with. Do not include your password.
  3. We confirm the request with you before acting on it. We tell you when it is done. The account's audit history is kept.

What is deleted

  • Your DigitVA account and sign-in credentials.
  • Your app device sessions, so the apps on your phones are signed out and cleared.
  • Your profile: name, email address and mobile number.
  • Interviews on a phone are held in encrypted form. Sent interviews are removed from the phone when the server confirms receipt. Unsent interviews stay on the phone, encrypted, after a sign-out or when a device is revoked, so they can still be sent; they are not deleted automatically. Deleting a demo account in the app also removes that account's data from the phone, unsent work included.

What is kept, and why

  • Verbal autopsy records about deceased persons, which are held for the programme under the respondent's consent. They are not personal data about your account. Where programme rules allow, they are separated from your account.
  • The account's audit history: records of actions taken in the system, which the programme needs to show that data were handled correctly. It is kept when a staff account is deleted. When a demo account deletes itself, its security and sign-in audit records are kept without its name (sign-in records keep the network address they came from); its other records go with the demo data.
  • Backups, until they expire in the normal backup cycle.

These are kept for the period the programme requires and applicable law allows. Contact us for the period that applies to you.
