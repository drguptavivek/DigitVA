Training and demo project

A permanent practice project where new staff learn every DigitVA role on realistic cases before they work on real ones.

Your own account. There are no shared or generated logins. You sign in with the account you already have, and your trainer adds you to a training session with a role. If you cannot see the training project after signing in, ask your trainer to add you.

The cases

The 200 cases in a training session come from verbal autopsy interviews whose participants gave their consent. Before they are copied, names, addresses and phone numbers in the text answers are replaced with made-up ones, consistently through each case. Everything else in the interview, such as symptoms, dates and ages, is kept so that coding is realistic. Coders see only their own 10 cases; the other roles see all the cases of their training session.

Photos, documents and audio are not changed. They are the original consented material and may show real details. Do not copy, photograph or share them.
